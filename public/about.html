// About me

Designer by training, researcher by accident.

Open to work Hyderabad · Remote Resume ↗

Five years in, and the thing I keep coming back to is behaviour. At Amazon I spent my days reading how people actually use a system — where they slip, what they misread, what they give up on — and then designing so the next person does not have to.

Before that: design systems and information architecture for corporate apps at O24U Nature, prototypes in Framer, and a long stretch of freelance branding that taught me more about typography than any course did.

How I think.

“Good design makes a product useful. It concentrates on certain criteria to admit, or rather not to impede, a product’s utility.”
~ Dieter Rams

Which, in practice, means I start with the logic and not the layout. Find the structure, prove it with people, then make it beautiful. In that order — the pretty part is easy once the thinking holds.
